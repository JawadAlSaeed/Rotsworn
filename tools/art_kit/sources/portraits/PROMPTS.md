# Big hero portraits: pictures to make with an AI image tool

Make one picture per hero with any AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, ...). Paste the text below.
Save each picture in this folder with exactly this name: `alder.png`, `brin.png`, `cass.png`
(a `.jpg` or `.webp` is fine too). `python tools/art_kit/portraits_from_pictures.py` then turns
them into the game's pixel art portraits.

Tip: if a picture comes out square, that is fine; the tool crops it to a tall frame around the
face.

## Alder

Character select portrait like the Metal Slug (Neo Geo, 1990s) select screen, but serious and
heroic, NOT goofy or a caricature: natural, good-looking proportions. Hand-painted pixel art
look, heavy dramatic shading, strong light from the upper left, warm tones, plain very dark
background. Tall portrait frame (2:3), head and shoulders, the face big and filling the frame,
looking a little to the right. Alder, a sturdy middle-aged warden: short brown hair, full thick
brown beard, kind but tired blue eyes, a broad nose, big steel shoulder plates over a deep blue
tabard, the gold rim of a large blue heater shield showing at his shoulder, a leather backpack
strap across his chest. Dark fantasy world. Not photo-realistic. No text, no frame, no border.
